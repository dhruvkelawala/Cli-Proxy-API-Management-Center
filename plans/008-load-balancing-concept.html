How priority and stickiness interact

Priority selects the highest eligible tier for a new assignment. The strategy chooses an account within that tier. An existing conversation keeps its eligible account, even after a higher-priority account recovers. With weights 3 and 1, the intended assignment ratio is roughly 75% and 25%; tokens, cost and elapsed time can differ.
